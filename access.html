Access & governance

Open by default, governed by design

Integrity is a property of every record, not just of the repository. Every value carries its lineage and an origin tag, and every release is gated, signed, and DOI'd.

Integrity

Every value carries its lineage and origin

Every file is SHA-256 hashed at ingest. Every derived product carries a W3C PROV lineage to the raw files, code, solver settings, agent prompts and models, and curator decisions behind it. AI pipelines can never silently train on model output as if it were measurement.

Policies

Written, public, and enforced

  • Licensing: CC-BY-4.0 for data; an OSI license for code.
  • Citation and credit: dataset DOIs and CRediT roles; contributors earn authorship on dataset descriptors.
  • Embargo: at most 12 months for contributors' unpublished work.
  • Retirement: a superseded or erroneous record is deprecated with a tombstone that keeps its DOI resolvable and states the reason. It is never silently deleted.
  • Privacy: no human-subjects data; incidental faces and plates in field imagery are blurred.
  • Audit: an annual external audit samples 5% of records and must find zero untagged synthetic values.
