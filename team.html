Team

Experiments, simulation, agents, and generative AI

Five PIs at Iowa State University, Georgia Tech, and Stanford University, with overlapping expertise that ties the aims together.

Principal Investigator · Icing experiments · the archive

Hui Hu

Anson Marston Distinguished Professor in Engineering and Martin C. Jischke Professor in Aerospace Engineering · Iowa State University

Has directed the ISU icing laboratory since 2006 and operates the ISU Icing Research Tunnel, studying aircraft and aero-engine icing physics and anti-/de-icing technology with advanced flow diagnostics. His experiments and UAV flight campaigns produced the archive at the core of FROST.

Leads in FROST
  • Aim 1.1 Feature extraction and metadata
  • Aim 1.2 Linkage and harmonization (co-lead)
  • Aim 3.2 Governance and contribution
Lab Homepage ISU profile Scholar
Co-PI · High-fidelity simulation

Suhas S. Jain

Assistant Professor of Mechanical Engineering (Aerospace Engineering by courtesy); Director, Center for Multiphase Flow Research · Georgia Institute of Technology

Models multiphase, turbulent, and compressible flows. His wall-modeled LES of iced aircraft is validated on laser-scanned IRT ice, and his ACDI phase-field method captures water films and droplets accurately and conservatively. NSF CAREER awardee.

Leads in FROST
  • Aim 1.3 Validated-simulation completion and augmentation
  • Discovery campaigns Q1–Q2
Lab Center Scholar GitHub
Co-PI · High-fidelity simulation · validation

Parviz Moin

Franklin P. and Caroline M. Johnson Professor in the School of Engineering; Founding Director, Center for Turbulence Research · Stanford University

Pioneered direct and large-eddy simulation for studying turbulence physics, control, and modeling; his current interests include large-eddy simulation for aerospace applications and aircraft icing. Member of the National Academy of Sciences, the National Academy of Engineering, and the American Academy of Arts and Sciences.

Leads in FROST
  • High-fidelity simulations of iced aircraft (with Jain, Aim 1.3)
  • Validation of the computations
  • Contributing simulation data to FROST
CTR Stanford profile
Co-PI · Optimization, planning & agents

Ping He

Assistant Professor of Aerospace Engineering · Iowa State University

Builds analysis, design, and optimization software for multidisciplinary systems, including the open-source adjoint framework DAFoam, and uses AI agents to make these tools faster and more robust. NSF CAREER awardee on autonomous AI agents for design optimization.

Leads in FROST
  • Aim 2.1 Agentic data pipelines
  • Aim 2.3 Discovery and applications
  • Aim 3.3 Availability and adoption
Lab ISU profile Scholar GitHub
Co-PI · Generative AI · data integrity

Han Gao

Assistant Professor of Aerospace Engineering · Iowa State University

Integrates AI and numerical methods for PDE-governed systems: diffusion models that forecast complex dynamics, generate turbulence, and solve parametric PDEs, and GPU high-order discontinuous-Galerkin LES of iced airfoils.

Leads in FROST
  • Aim 2.2 FROST-Gen generative model
  • Aim 3.1 Security, integrity, and access
  • Aim 1.2 Linkage and harmonization (co-lead)

Where the expertise overlaps

A team that already works together

Hu × Jain

Scanned ice and IRT data validate LES; a neural operator for icing water films (JFM, 2026).

Jain × Moin

Wall-modeled LES of iced airfoils and wings, droplet impingement, and conjugate heat transfer on laser-scanned IRT ice, coauthored by Jain and Moin.

Jain × He

LES and DAFoam simulations; agents set up validated cases.

He × Gao

PhysMiner agents, co-developed; AI-driven design.

Hu × He

A shared NSF icing award and machine-learning studies of wind-driven runback.

Gao × Hu

A shared study of the icing-induced penalty on the S1223 UAV wing, and the NASA IRT 401–405 ice-accretion model.

Governance

A Steering Committee of the PIs plus external members from aircraft icing, UAS operations, certification, and AI for science; a Data Curation Board reviews releases.

Training & outreach

Students at the center

Undergraduates in AI-driven use of scientific data, course modules at ISU and Georgia Tech, and K–12 tours of the icing tunnel with a FROST station where an AI model predicts the ice shape before the tunnel grows it.
